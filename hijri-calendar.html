Islamic Calendar

The Hijri calendar is the lunar calendar used by Muslims to determine important Islamic dates, months and occasions.

🌙 The Hijri calendar has 12 lunar months.
🕋 Important Islamic occasions follow the Hijri calendar.
📅 Dates may vary depending on the moon-sighting or calculation method.
