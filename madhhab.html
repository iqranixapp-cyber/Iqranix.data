ℹ️ About Madhhabs

A Madhhab is a recognized school of Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh). In Iqranix, this setting is used only to calculate the time of the Asr prayer according to your preferred method. It does not affect your beliefs, worship, or any other prayer times.

🕌 Shafi'i Method

The Shafi'i method is widely used in many parts of Africa, Southeast Asia, East Africa, Yemen, Egypt, and by many Islamic organizations worldwide. It calculates Asr when the length of an object's shadow equals its height (excluding the noon shadow).

🕌 Hanafi Method

The Hanafi method is followed by many Muslims in South Asia, Türkiye, Central Asia, and other regions. It calculates Asr when the length of an object's shadow becomes twice its height (excluding the noon shadow).

Note: Changing your Madhhab in Iqranix only changes the calculation of the Asr prayer time. All other prayer times remain unchanged.
