ℹ️ About Adhan Volume

The selected volume is used whenever Iqranix plays an offline Adhan. The actual loudness may also depend on your device's current media volume and system settings.

💡 Recommendation

For the best experience, keep your device's media volume above 70% and allow Iqranix to play offline Adhan sounds without battery restrictions. This helps ensure the Adhan can be heard clearly at prayer time.
