1. Introduction

Welcome to Iqranix, an Islamic digital platform developed to provide users with access to beneficial Islamic resources and practical tools intended to assist them in their daily worship, learning, remembrance and engagement with the Qur'an and the broader Islamic tradition.

We regard privacy, dignity, trust and responsible stewardship of information as matters of serious importance. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, stored, used and protected when you access or use the Iqranix application, website, Progressive Web App, or related services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using Iqranix, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, you should discontinue use of the Service.

2. Our Privacy Principles

Iqranix seeks to operate according to principles of proportionality, necessity, transparency and respect for personal dignity. We endeavour to collect and process only information reasonably required to provide, maintain, secure and improve the Service.

In keeping with the Islamic principle of amanah — the responsibility to safeguard what has been entrusted to us — we seek to treat information entrusted to Iqranix with appropriate care and discretion.

3. Information We May Process

Depending upon the features you choose to use, Iqranix may process certain categories of information necessary for the proper operation of the Service.

3.1 Device and Technical Information

Certain technical information may be processed automatically by the application, browser, hosting provider, analytics services, security systems or other infrastructure supporting the Service. Such information may include device type, operating system, browser characteristics, approximate technical identifiers, network information and diagnostic information.

3.2 Location Information

Certain Iqranix features, particularly prayer-time calculations, Qibla-related functionality and location-sensitive Islamic services, may require access to your device's location.

Location access is requested through the appropriate device permission mechanisms. You may withdraw location permission through your device settings, although doing so may prevent certain location-dependent features from functioning correctly.

3.3 Notification Information

If you enable notifications, Iqranix may use the information necessary to schedule or deliver prayer reminders, Adhan notifications and other notifications that you have expressly enabled.

3.4 Preferences and Local Storage

Certain preferences may be stored locally on your device. These may include notification preferences, selected Adhan sounds, prayer adjustments, bookmarks, application settings and other configuration information.

Local storage is principally used to preserve your chosen settings and improve the continuity of your experience.

Information processed in connection with Iqranix may be used for legitimate purposes including:
