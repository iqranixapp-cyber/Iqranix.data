General

What is Iqranix?

Iqranix is an Islamic companion application designed to help Muslims strengthen their connection with Allah ﷻ through the Holy Qur'an, prayer times, du'as, Islamic reminders, Qiblah, educational resources, and other beneficial tools.

Cost

Is Iqranix free to use?

Yes. The core features of Iqranix are available free of charge. Additional features may be introduced in future versions while keeping the app accessible to the Muslim Ummah.

Prayer Times

Why are my prayer times different from my local mosque?

Prayer times are calculated using recognised calculation methods based on your location. Your local mosque may apply different calculation methods or local adjustments, so slight differences are normal.

Qiblah

How accurate is the Qiblah direction?

The Qiblah compass uses your device's GPS and compass sensors. For the best accuracy, ensure that location services are enabled and calibrate your device's compass if necessary.

Holy Qur'an

Can I read the complete Holy Qur'an?

Yes. Iqranix allows you to read all 114 Surahs of the Holy Qur'an together with translations where available.

Audio

Can I listen to Qur'an recitations?

Yes. Iqranix supports beautiful Qur'an recitations from trusted reciters. An internet connection may be required for streaming audio unless offline support is added in a future release.

Bookmarks

Can I save my favourite Surahs and du'as?

Yes. Iqranix allows you to bookmark your favourite Surahs, verses, and other content so that you can quickly return to them later.

Internet

Does Iqranix require an internet connection?

Some features work offline, while others—such as streaming Qur'an audio, online updates, maps, and certain external services—require an active internet connection.

Privacy

Does Iqranix collect my personal data?

Iqranix respects your privacy. Any information collected is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the Services.

Notifications

Can I disable reminders and notifications?

Yes. You can enable or disable reminders, prayer notifications, and other alerts at any time from the Settings page according to your personal preferences.

Updates

How do I receive new features?

New features, improvements, security updates, and bug fixes are delivered through app updates. We recommend keeping Iqranix updated to enjoy the latest improvements and enhancements.

Report a Problem

I found a bug. What should I do?

If you discover an error, incorrect information, or experience a technical issue, please contact the Iqranix Team with as much detail as possible. Your feedback helps us improve the app for everyone.
