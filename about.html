About Iqranix

Iqranix is a purpose-driven digital Islamic companion designed to bring beneficial Islamic resources, worship tools, learning experiences, and meaningful reminders together within one accessible platform.

Faith, Knowledge & Excellence

Iqranix is founded upon the principles of beneficial knowledge, sincere worship, personal reflection, responsible technology, and continuous improvement.

Our objective is to provide Muslims with thoughtfully designed digital tools that can assist them in maintaining a meaningful connection with the Qur'an, prayer, remembrance, learning, and other aspects of Islamic life.

Our Mission

The mission of Iqranix is to make beneficial Islamic resources more accessible through thoughtful, reliable, and user-focused digital technology.

We seek to create an environment in which technology serves as a means of remembrance, learning, reflection, and personal development rather than becoming a distraction from the responsibilities and values of everyday life.

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish Iqranix as a trusted digital Islamic companion that Muslims can use throughout their daily lives, wherever they may be.

We aspire to develop a platform that combines accessibility, elegant design, useful technology, educational resources, and respect for Islamic principles.

The Vision Behind Iqranix

Iqranix is being developed with the vision of bringing Qur'an, worship, remembrance, Islamic knowledge, community, and beneficial digital tools together in one modern Islamic companion.

“The goal is not simply to build an app, but to build something beneficial.”

What Iqranix Provides

Iqranix is designed to bring a range of Islamic resources and practical worship tools together in one convenient application.

  • Holy Qur'an: Access Qur'anic reading and recitation resources designed to support regular engagement with the Book of Allah.
  • Prayer Times: Daily Salah schedules based upon the user's geographical location.
  • Qibla: A digital direction-finding tool intended to assist users in determining the direction of the Ka'bah.
  • Adhan & Prayer Reminders: Configurable reminders designed to help users remember their daily prayers.
  • Adhkar & Du'as: Resources intended to encourage remembrance of Allah and regular supplication.
  • Tasbih: A digital counting tool for personal dhikr sessions.
  • Islamic Calendar: Information relating to the Hijri calendar and significant Islamic occasions.
  • Islamic Reminders: Thoughtful reminders intended to encourage beneficial habits and reflection.
  • Community: Features intended to help Muslims connect, participate, learn, and share beneficial content in a respectful environment.
  • Charitable Initiatives: Where appropriate partnerships or verified fundraising opportunities are available, Iqranix may provide users with access to charitable initiatives intended to support people in need.
  • Additional Islamic Resources: Iqranix may introduce further educational, devotional, and community-oriented features as the platform develops.

Our Islamic Principles

Iqranix is intended to support the practice and study of Islam while maintaining humility regarding the limits of digital technology.

We encourage users to consult qualified scholars, teachers, and recognised Islamic institutions whenever a matter requires detailed religious clarification, scholarly interpretation, or a formal Islamic ruling.

Digital tools can assist a person's worship and learning; they do not replace qualified scholarship, personal responsibility, or sincere engagement with authentic Islamic sources.

Qur'an & Hadith

The Qur'an occupies the central place in the spiritual life of a Muslim. Iqranix therefore seeks to make Qur'anic engagement convenient through reading, recitation, memorisation-oriented tools, and related educational features.

Where Hadith and other Islamic educational materials are presented, we aim to treat attribution, authenticity, context, and intellectual responsibility with appropriate care.

Users should nevertheless verify important religious matters against authoritative primary sources and qualified scholarship.

Responsible Technology

We believe technology should be developed with responsibility, transparency, and respect for the people who use it.

Iqranix is designed with privacy considerations in mind, while aiming to provide useful functionality without unnecessarily encouraging unhealthy dependence upon digital technology.
